A CLEAR VIEW OF YOUR SETTINGS

Cookies, without the mystery.

A short guide to the small files this site may use, what they do, and how you can manage them.

LAST UPDATED 6 November 2025

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files a website can store in your browser. Some are needed for basic functions, such as keeping a secure administrator session. Others can remember preferences or measure site usage.

This independent site turns DPSA circulars and other government vacancy notices into searchable listings. We use essential cookies for site security and administrator sign-in, Google Analytics cookies to measure site usage, and Cloudflare Turnstile to help protect forms against automated abuse. Separate aggregate search and page-view counts do not use cookies or identify individual visitors.

COOKIE CATEGORIES

What’s active on this site

We’ll update this statement if our cookie use changes.

01IN USE

Essential

Necessary for site security and administrator sign-in. Supabase authentication may use session cookies when an administrator logs in. The public vacancy pages do not require an account.

02NOT IN USE

Preferences

The site does not currently use cookies to save preferred provinces, departments, search filters, or language choices.

03IN USE

Analytics

Google Analytics (measurement ID G-69H0HQGRT9) measures site usage and may use analytics cookies. Separate anonymous daily counts of searches and page views are stored without cookies or visitor identifiers.

04NOT IN USE

Advertising & other third parties

The site does not use advertising or third-party tracking cookies. Cloudflare Turnstile provides security checks on forms and is not used for advertising. Following an external job application link takes you to another site with its own cookie practices.

YOUR CHOICES

Managing cookies in your browser

Most browsers let you view, block, or delete cookies in their privacy or security settings. Blocking essential cookies may prevent administrator sign-in or other secure site functions from working correctly. Blocking analytics cookies may affect Google Analytics measurement.
